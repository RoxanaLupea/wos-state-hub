# Alliance Mobilization

Sent 22 Nov 2026.

## Participation Requirements
1. Before the event starts, the alliance needs more than 15 members and Furnace Lv. 10 to
   enter.
2. Changing or quitting alliances during the event means the rewards cannot be claimed.
3. At least one member of the alliance must sign in within the week before the event
   starts.

## Event Rules
1. A new season starts every four weeks.
2. The event page is open to chiefs with Furnace Lv. 10 and above in an alliance with more
   than 15 members.
3. Points from members completing event missions add up as Alliance Points. Points belong
   to the starting alliance; changing alliance removes the right to take part this season.
4. Chiefs can refresh exclusive missions not yet accepted; only 1 exclusive mission a day
   gives 200% Alliance Points. R4 and above can refresh public missions.
5. Abandoning an accepted mission uses up the acceptance attempt. Quitting the alliance
   during the event abandons all accepted or completed missions.
6. A Custom Mission Voucher gives a choice of exclusive missions worth double points the
   first time each day; later ones with a voucher give 120%. If the event ends before the
   mission is completed, 1 voucher is refunded by mail.
7. Alliance rewards are not given to members who leave before the tally phase. Personal
   Ranking Rewards are tallied when the ranking Display Phase starts. Rewards are tallied
   on Sundays at 00:00 UTC.

## League Overview
1. Alliances collect Event Points to rise in the League tiers; higher tiers give better
   rewards.
2. Tiers from lowest to highest: Rookie, Junior, Senior, Elite, Legendary. First-time
   alliances start in Rookie. Alliances that reach milestone level 8 and rank top 3 in
   their tier move up.
3. The last 3 in each tier move down in the next season. Rookie is the lowest tier.
4. On a tie, the chief or alliance that reached the points first ranks higher.
