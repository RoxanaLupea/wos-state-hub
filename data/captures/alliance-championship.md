# Alliance Championship Overview

Sent 27 Nov 2026 by the owner (5 screenshots: Rules, Rewards: Tier Rewards for Ultimate and KO
Rewards). Everything on the site's Rules and Rewards tabs matches these; the one rule the site
does not have is noted at the end.

## Rules

Battle Prerequisites
1. You must be part of an Alliance.
2. The Chief's Furnace must reach Lv.10.
3. At least 10 Alliance members must also register.

[Sign-Up Phase]
1. Interested Chiefs must proceed to the [Alliance Championship] sign-up area to sign up, then
   select a battle lane and place troops.
2. Troops deployed into an Alliance Championship battle do not use March Slots and troops will
   NOT be killed.
3. Only a certain number of troops in each lane can do battle at one time. The troops with the
   highest Power will battle first.
4. All Alliance members that signed up for the Alliance Championship may receive rewards,
   including those that have not battled.
5. An Alliance cannot take part if too few Alliance members sign up for the event.

[Matchmaking Phase]
1. Matchmaking will randomly assign 6 different Alliances of the same or a very similar tier
   into one group.
2. Once registered, an Alliance cannot disband before the end of the Championship.
3. If an Alliance member signs up but later decides to exit the Competition during the
   Matchmaking Phase, their troops will continue to take part, but with no reward given.

[Preparation Phase]
1. The Championship follows a round robin format matching all Alliances in a given group
   against each other in a series of 5 showdowns.
2. The Alliance Leader and R4 or higher officers can freely switch troop deployment between the
   three lanes of battle or swap single armies assigned in a lane before each round's showdown
   begins (Preparation Phase).

[Battle Phase]
1. The Alliance on each side must attempt to capture 3 flags on 3 lanes once the battle begins.
2. The troops on each side of a lane will take turns fighting against each other, with
   victorious survivors battling against the opponent's next round of troops. However, each
   individual army only gets to fight twice.
3. The first Alliance to defeat all enemy troops in a given lane will be declared the lane
   winner and receive that lane's flag.
4. Whichever side gains the most flags is the showdown winner and earns 3 points.
5. In the event of a draw, when each side gains the same number of flags, both sides earn 1
   point.
6. In the Championship, only expedition-type hero skills take effect.

[Completion Phase]
1. Each alliance is given a performance-based ranking within the group and rewards after the
   5-round showdown is completed (except for players awarded auto-advance victory rewards, who
   are not eligible for further ranking rewards).
2. Alliances in each group are also ranked based on the number of Championship Points earned.
   In the event of multiple alliances earning the same number of Championship Points, Rankings
   depend on the number of alliance flags captured and the enemies defeated.
3. Alliance rewards will be distributed at UTC 12:00 every Friday through mail, please take
   note. Alliance rewards will only be distributed to members in the alliance during tally, and
   not members who leave before it.

## Rewards: Tier Rewards (Ultimate, current tier)

Prestige Rewards: four items (as on the site). Group Ranking, Star Rating, Rewards (5-min
speedups, resource chests, the third item):
- 1: +2 stars; 63, 830, 23.8K
- 2~3: +1; 55, 720, 19K
- 4~5: -1; 48, 625, 14.3K
- 6: -2; 41, 545, 9,500

## Rewards: KO Rewards

Rules Overview
1. KO Rewards will be assigned to all participants at the conclusion of each battle round in the
   round-robin phase, with reward size based on the number of teams vanquished.
2. Troops to defeat 1 enemy team will earn Grade 1 Rewards, troops to defeat 2 enemy teams will
   earn Grade 2 Rewards.
3. Undefeated combatants will earn Grade 2 Rewards.
4. During preparation before each round, the Alliance Leader and R4 or higher officers can
   switch the teams on each lane. This might cause some Chiefs to be excluded from the
   combatant list (the top 20 most powerful Chiefs). Only Chiefs on this list are eligible for
   rewards.

- KO Reward 1: purple chest x1, 5-min research speedup x10, resource chest x80.
- KO Reward 2: purple chest x1, 5-min research speedup x15, resource chest x120.
- Defeat Rewards: 5-min research speedup x10, 10K meat x20, 10K wood x20, 1K coal x40, 1K iron
  x10.
