## Seasons
The game's Guide rule: "Seasonal Fishing: events are cyclical, corresponding to the four
seasons." It does not give the order. The Guide tab showed "Current season: Summer" for the
run of 24–27 Nov 2026 (the owner's screenshot, 27 Nov).
Source outside the game, Century Games' help centre (FAQ 6640, checked 27 Nov 2026): the
season changes in the order winter, spring, summer, fall. So the next run is autumn.

## Tokens and Points
1. Join the Fishing Tournament while in season to earn Fishing Points and Fishing Tokens.
2. Fishing Points grant Club rewards and a higher ranking on the Master Fisher Leaderboard.
3. Fishing Tokens can be redeemed for Fishing Kit upgrades and Sunken Treasure Hunts.
   Every 10 unused Tokens are converted into 10K Meat at the end of the event.

## Fishing Stage
1. Ice Fishing: 1 Bait per attempt. You start with 5 Baits and receive 1 more every 3
   hours, up to a maximum of 10.
2. Frosty Prospector: an exotic area with rare fish and Sunken Chests, 1 Treasure Chart
   per attempt. Fishing kits used there come with maxed-out stats.
3. The phone vibrates while the sinker descends; it can be turned off in Settings, Game
   Settings.

## Kit Upgrades
1. Upgrade the Fishing Kit with Fishing Tokens.
2. Line: raises maximum line depth.
3. Hook: raises maximum haul per attempt.
4. Sinker: raises hook starting depth.
5. The Fishing Kit is automatically maxed when the event begins.

## Special Items
1. Icefisher Voucher: 1.5x Fishing Token and Fishing Point reward bonus in the
   end-of-round tally.
2. Horn of Poseidon: summons a specific type of target.
3. Reel Stabilizer: stops the line reeling in when a fish is hit on the way down; protects
   it two more times.
4. Ocean Scanner: monitors nearby fish and signals when rare species are present.
5. Lantern: visibility drops at depths of 300 m; the Lantern lights the surroundings.
6. Unused special items carry over to the next event session.
7. Items selected before a level are deducted whether used or not. In Ice Fishing no
   Lantern is needed if the Line is level 4 or below.

## Guide
1. Guide types: Fish Guide, Diver Guide, Chest Guide and Memorabilia Guide. New types
   become available as you explore.
2. Seasonal Fishing: events follow the four seasons. Some fish, divers, chests and
   memorabilia appear only in certain seasons. Completed objectives are saved
   permanently and do not reset with the seasons.
3. Star Rating Rewards: objectives in each Guide carry a star rating; completing them
   unlocks better Star Rating rewards.
4. Guide Completion Rewards: completing all objectives in a Guide gives special Skin
   rewards.
5. Chests obtained while fishing go to the backpack directly.

## Master Fisher Leaderboard
1. Fish to earn Fishing Points and a higher ranking during the event.
2. Chiefs are ranked and rewarded by Fishing Points at the end of the event.
3. Leaderboard rewards include special Ice Fishing Skins.

## Ice Fishing Club
1. Fish to earn Fishing Points and level up during the event.
2. Higher membership tiers unlock Fishing Token Bonuses and Fishing Point Bonuses.
3. The bonuses from Fishing Pro and Fishing Master stack.
4. Unclaimed rewards are mailed after the event.
5. Each pack can be bought only once per event.

## Sunken Treasure Hunt
1. Use Fishing Tokens to hunt for treasure.
2. It shows once the total Fishing Kit level reaches 25, and unlocks once the whole kit is
   maxed.
3. Every 10 unused Fishing Tokens become 10K Meat at the end of the event.

## Power Cast
1. Power Cast is available this season to boost Fishing Point and Token earning.
2. With Power Cast, Ice Fishing and Frosty Prospector use as many baits and Treasure
   Charts as the multiplier selected. Special items are not affected by the multiplier.
3. Icefisher Vouchers add to the selected multiplier (including Ice Fishing Club and Diver
   bonuses). Several can be used at once, up to the selected multiplier. Example from the
   game: 1,000 points at 1x, Power Cast 5x with 3 vouchers (0.5x each) gives
   1000 × (3 × 0.5 + 5) = 6,500.
4. Sunken Chests obtained are multiplied by the selected Power Cast multiplier.

## Fishing Probability Guide
1. Ice Fishing
   - Fish: 5-Star 1 fish 10%; 4-Star 1 fish 100%; 3-Star 2 fish 30%, 3 fish 30%, 4 fish
     20%, 5 fish 20%; remaining fish 2-Star 50%, 1-Star 50%.
   - Divers: 2-Star 1 non-struggling diver 100%, 1 struggling diver 3.5%; 3-Star 1 diver
     1.5%.
   - Chests: 4-Star 1 chest 3%; 3-Star 1 chest 10%; 2-Star 1 chest 50%.
   - Memorabilia: 3-Star 1 memorabilia 5%; other memorabilia 2-Star 50%, 1-Star 50%.
   - Seaweed: 5-Star 1 seaweed 2%; 4-Star 20%; 3-Star 50%; 2-Star 100%.
2. Frosty Prospector
   - Fish: 5-Star 1 fish 100%; 4-Star 1 fish 50%, 2 fish 50%; remaining fish 3-Star 30%,
     2-Star 35%, 1-Star 35%.
   - Divers: 2-Star 1 non-struggling diver 50%, 1 struggling diver 14%; 3-Star 1 diver 6%.
   - Chests: 4-Star 1 chest 20%; 3-Star 1 chest 100%; 2-Star 1 chest 100%.
   - Memorabilia: 3-Star 1 memorabilia 100%; remaining 2-Star 50%, 1-Star 50%.
   - Seaweed: 5-Star 1 seaweed 50%; 4-Star 100%; 3-Star 100%; 2-Star 100%.
3. Depth and season also affect the probabilities.

## Fishing Pro Set
The Fishing Pro Set may be available outside Fishing Tournament times. Leftover Fishing
Tokens are kept as the Tokens and Points rules say after the tournament ends; other items
carry over seasonally.
