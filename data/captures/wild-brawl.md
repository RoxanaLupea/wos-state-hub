# Wild Brawl

Sent 22 Nov 2026 (guide, reward details, Icebreaker Shop and battle screenshots).

## Guide
1. The Icebreaker Alliance has launched a brawl: face heroes from other generations.
2. The Twilight Citadel and three Boulder Fortresses stand at the centre of the
   battlefield. Holding them earns a steady stream of Strategic Points, which decide the
   ranking.
3. 30 seconds after the Preparation Phase ends, three supply trucks appear from the edge of
   the battlefield, with more every minute. They follow set paths.
4. Supply trucks randomly drop Strategic Supply Chests along the way. Squads collect them
   for Strategic Points.
5. 90 seconds after the Preparation Phase ends, a transport aircraft flies from the edge
   toward the centre, dropping item crates. More arrive every 2 minutes.
6. Airdrops are Destroyer Rockets, Frost Bombs, Battlefield Medkits and Smoke Bombs.
   Collecting them gives extra Strategic Points.

## Map
Twilight Citadel in the centre, three Boulder Fortresses around it (Boulder Fortress I and
III seen by name), and three crater areas where the trucks drop chests. A battle showed a
timer of about 10 minutes.
