# Frostdragon Tyrant: Reward Details

Sent 22 Nov 2026. The Point Rewards tab has not been sent yet.

## Frostdragon Tyrant Rewards
For the chief who holds the Capital longest (30 days).

### Tyrant Exclusive Skin (30-Day, auto-applied)
1. Dragonhold, city skin: Troops' Attack +5.0%.
2. Frost Dragon, march skin: Hunting March Speed +10.0%.
3. A third skin (snowy landscape picture): name and bonus not seen.
4. Dragonis Dominion, chief profile skin: Troops' Health +2.5%.
5. Frostwing Avatar Frame: Troops' Defense +3%.
6. Frostwing Nameplate.

### Tyrant Exclusive Pack
Dragon Pagoda Lv. 1 ×3; 1,000 Gems ×50; Mithril ×5; red-and-gold gear chest ×1; grey chest
with "?" ×20; 8h General Speedup ×8; 100 Enhancement XP Component ×50; purple-tile
resource chest with "?" ×100.

The red-and-gold chest matches the Custom Mythic Hero Gear Chest picture but its name was
not shown. The grey chest looks like the Lucky Hero Gear Chest as named in the Commendation
tooltips; not confirmed.

### Tyrant Exclusive Title
Stacks with other title bonuses: Title-based Troops' Attack +10.00%, Defense +10.00%,
Lethality +10.00%, Health +10.00%; Troops Deployment Capacity +5,000.

### Appoint Title
The Frostdragon Tyrant may grant four titles to chiefs under State jurisdiction (stack with
other titles): Bloodwyrm Emissary, Firebreather Vanguard, Scaled Guardian, Dragontooth
Governor. Their bonuses were not shown.

### Commendation Chest
The Tyrant may grant a limited number of three Commendation Chest types to chiefs under
State jurisdiction.

Item details from the game:
- Triumph of Tyrant: a symbol of supreme prestige for the top chief who holds the capital
  longest; gives 5 Honor Points and shows on the Chief Profile. Source: Frostdragon Tyrant.
- Serpent Sanctuary Lv. 1: 4x4, Mythic, type limit 1, 5,000; Troops' Attack, Defense,
  Lethality and Health +2.0% each. Stored in the Island Storehouse when obtained.
- Dragon Pagoda Lv. 1: 3x3, Mythic, type limit 1, 1,000; Troops' Attack +1.0%. Stored in
  the Island Storehouse when obtained.
