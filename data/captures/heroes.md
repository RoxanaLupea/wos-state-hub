# Heroes

## Hero list (sent 24 Nov 2026, from the owner's account)

- The Heroes screen, sorted by Power, with Drill Camp and Recruit Hero at the bottom.
- Each hero card shows the class icon in the top left (the same shield, spear head and bow
  as the camp tabs), a mark such as S5 or S8 in the top right on some cards, "Lv. 80"
  bottom left, and five stars along the bottom.
- The stars are six-petal stars. A full star has all six petals lit blue; an empty star
  is brown. A star in progress has some petals lit, starting from the top petal and
  going counter-clockwise.
- This matches the game data from Whiteout Stats: a hero's star value runs from 0 to 30,
  six steps to a star, labelled for example "4-Star (Tier 3)" for four full stars and
  three petals of the fifth.

## Hero cards (sent 24 Nov 2026, four Heroes screenshots, from the owner's account)

- 50 heroes: legendary on orange cards, epic on purple, rare on blue.
- Each card is cut out and cleaned by tools/mockups/player-page-v2/clean_hero_cards.py:
  the level, the stars, the S mark and the red dot are taken off (the S mark and the red
  dot belong to the owner's account), and the art fades into the card's own colour at the
  bottom where the site writes the stars. The class badge stays.
- 37 cards are saved in public/images/heroes/cards/ under the hero's name, matched
  against the portraits already in public/images/heroes/.
- 13 cards (9 epic, 4 rare) have no portrait to match and are not saved yet; their names
  are still to come from the owner.
