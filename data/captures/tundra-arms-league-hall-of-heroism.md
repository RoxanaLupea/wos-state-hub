## The game's rules screen (Hall of Heroism)

1. Chiefs can view Hall of Heroism information, Honored Legions, Season MVPs and Final MVPs for
   each Zone for the current or previous seasons.
2. Honored Legions refers to champion, runner-up, and third-place Legions. For the top 4 Legions,
   all Chiefs at Furnace Level 20 or higher in their States is entitled to claim State Rewards.
   Glory is meant to be shared.
3. There are several categories of Season MVP: Season MVP (main category), as well as Season MVP
   by Occupation Points, Gathering Points, Loot Points, or KO Points.
   - 3.1 Season MVP: The Chief to earn the most Personal Points in a single match over the course
     of a season.
   - 3.2 Season Occupation MVP: The Chief to earn the most Personal Occupation Points in a single
     match over the course of a season.
   - 3.3 Season Gathering MVP: The Chief to earn the most Personal Gathering Points in a single
     match over the course of a season.
   - 3.4 Season Loot MVP: the Chief to earn the most Personal Loot Points in a single match over
     the course of a season.
   - 3.5 Season KO MVP: The Chief to earn the most Personal KO Points in a single match over the
     course of a season.
   - Note: If there is more than one Chief with the same number of points in any category, MVP will
     be awarded based on whichever is greatest of the Chiefs' next best achievement.
4. Final MVP refers to the Final MVP (main category), as well as Final MVP by Occupation Points,
   Gathering Points, Loot Points, and KO points.
   - 4.1 Final MVP: The Chief to earn the most Personal Points in a final match.
   - 4.2 Final Occupation MVP: The Chief to earn the most Personal Occupation Points in a final
     match.
   - 4.3 Final Gathering MVP: The Chief to earn the most Personal Gathering Points in a final match.
   - 4.4 Final Loot MVP: The Chief to earn the most Personal Loot Points in a final match.
   - 4.5 Final KO MVP: The Chief to earn the most Personal KO Points in a final match.
   - Note: Where two or more Chiefs tie for MVP in any category, the award will go to whichever
     reached the relevant score first.

## The screens' names

- Tabs: Honored Legion, Seasonal MVP, Final MVP. MVP cards: MVP, Occupation MVP, Gathering MVP,
  Looting MVP, Kill MVP.
- The only round the game names here is the Final ("a final match").

## How the Championship runs (Roxanne, 27 Nov)

"#1 and #2 are fighting, winner goes to the final, loser has to fight the winner of #3 vs #4, the
loser gets the #4th rank, their points decides from which position they start, then the loser of
the golden fights the winner and the winner of the 2 fights the winner of the golden in the final."
