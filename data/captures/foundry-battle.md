# Foundry Battle

Source: Roxanne's game screenshots, sent 27 Nov 2026 (the event's Guide, 8 pages, and Rules
Overview, Rules and Other tabs), kept on the screenshots branch in foundry-battle/. The map with
the buildings' names came with them. Guide pages 2, 5, 6, 7 and 8 are cut off at the bottom; the
Building Details and Rewards tabs were not sent yet.

## The field (map)
Imperial Foundry in the centre; Mercenary above it and Munitions below it; Prototype 1 and 2;
Repair 1 to 4; Boiler; Transit.

## Guide
1. Introduction only.
2. The Imperial Foundry is a key battleground (rest cut off).
3. Controlling the Imperial Foundry in the centre earns a continuous stream of Arsenal Points.
   Arsenal Points are the key to victory.
4. Two Prototype Sites near the Imperial Foundry also earn Arsenal Points.
5. A Repair Facility generates Arsenal Points. Controlling the Munitions Warehouse boosts your
   troops in combat. Controlling the Boiler Room reduces the time ... (cut off).
6. Occupied buildings produce Arsenal Points until they reach capacity. Of the points over
   capacity, 50% go straight to the occupier's Arsenal Points and 50% to the occupier's temporary
   Arsenal Points (cut off).
7. Temporary Arsenal Points from a building are lost by the defender when an attacker takes
   control. They are strewn around as Arsenal Supplies, which either side can loot for points.
8. Hidden Weapon Workshops appear on the field from time to time. Soldiers sent to loot them earn
   Arsenal Points. Workshops appear in two waves (cut off).

## Rules
- Troops cannot die in Foundry Battle and heal on their own at the end of the battle.
- Two alliances are matched. Taking control of a building for the first time, or keeping control
  of it, earns Alliance and Personal Arsenal Points. The battle ends when the countdown reaches
  zero. The alliance with the most Alliance Arsenal Points wins; on a tie, the alliance that
  killed more enemy troops wins.
- Rewards come from the alliance's Overall Performance and each Chief's Personal Performance.
- Alliance tiers and rewards come from the Legion 1 battle.
- An alliance that does not register for 3 seasons in a row or more is left off the Win Ratio
  Ranking.

### Procedures
1. Registration: the leader and R4s of alliances in the top 20 of the Alliance Power Leaderboard
   sign up. Members who join after sign-up has ended cannot take part and cannot earn the
   alliance participation rewards. Each alliance can register two Legions. When sign-up ends,
   the participant list is locked. Members R4 and below can request any Legion and switch until
   sign-up ends, one Legion at a time.
2. Combatants: up to 30 Combatants and 10 Substitutes, chosen by the leader and R4s. A member can
   be picked for one Legion only, as Combatant or Substitute.
3. Matchmaking: Legions are matched on Matchmaking Points, the Troop Power of the top 20
   registered Chiefs (from Chief Gear, Hero Gear, Battle Tech, Pet, Expert Power, Furnace Level and
   Troop Level). Legions registered for the same time slot are matched first; if none fits, a
   Legion of another time slot. The final match time is picked at random from either side's
   chosen slots or the midpoint between the two.
4. Entering the battlefield: once the battle begins, with no troops scouting or marching outside
   the City, no injured troops in the Infirmary, no burning Barricades and no reinforcements in
   the Embassy.
5. On the field allies are blue and enemies red.
6. Phases: 1, Prototype Sites, Repair Facilities, Boiler Rooms and Transit Stations can be
   contested; 2, Imperial Foundries, Munitions Warehouses and Mercenary Camps; 3, Weapon Workshops
   appear at random across the map.

## Other
### Battle Prerequisites
- The top 20 alliances on the Power Leaderboard before the event starts can take part.
- Legion 1 is unlocked by default. Legion 2 needs the alliance at Lv. 6 or higher with 40 members
  who logged in this week; members leaving lower the count.
- A Legion needs at least 15 Combatants and Substitutes together for matchmaking, counted per
  Legion.
- Members who joined before registration with a Furnace of Lv. 16 or higher can take part.
- Members offline for 5 days or more cannot be dispatched, unless already dispatched.

### Fight
- Occupying or controlling buildings earns Alliance and Personal Arsenal Points and the
  building's buffs.
- Each Troop of 10,000 Total Troop Power defeated while defending earns 40 Personal Arsenal
  Points, while attacking 80; neither earns Alliance Arsenal Points.
- Combatants get 1 free Advanced Teleporter at the start, recovered for free after its cooldown.
- Troops go to an unlimited, free Infirmary and recover on leaving the field.
- Cities burn faster on the field; a City whose defenders are beaten and whose fire is not put out
  quickly is moved to a safe zone. A Chief forced out can rejoin after a 12-minute cooldown.
- Tech, VIP, Chief Gear and Skin bonuses stay active.
